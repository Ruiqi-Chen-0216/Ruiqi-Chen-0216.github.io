Combine selected Google calendars in a desktop month, week, day, or agenda view. Google events remain read-only.

Plan time together

Create shared Together events inside the app. These events stay separate from Google Calendar.

Share across devices

Optionally relay encrypted event copies through a private GitHub repository to devices configured with the same pairing key.

Why the app asks for Google access

Google sign-in identifies the account you choose to connect. Read-only Calendar access lets the app list calendars and display their events, including titles, times, locations, descriptions, and attendees. It does not create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

When device sync is enabled, selected calendar information is encrypted and shared with the other configured devices. Connect an account only if you are authorized to share those calendars with the people using those devices.

Personal use, by invitation. Maintained by Ruiqi Chen for personal use and people he explicitly authorizes. This website provides app information; it does not display calendars or offer a public sign-up.

Your information and choices

OAuth credentials stay on the computer that authorizes the account. Calendar copies are cached locally for offline viewing. You can disconnect Google access, choose which calendars to share, and disable device sync.

Read the Privacy Policy for the full data flow and deletion limitations, and the Terms of Use for how the app is intended to be used.
